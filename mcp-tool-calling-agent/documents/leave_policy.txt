ACME TECHNOLOGIES INC.
LEAVE POLICY DOCUMENT
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026
==================================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
ACME Technologies provides various types of leave to support employee
well-being and work-life balance. This policy applies to all full-time
employees. Part-time and contract employees may have different entitlements.

2. ANNUAL / VACATION LEAVE
---------------------------
Full-time employees are entitled to:
- 0-2 years of service: 15 days per year
- 2-5 years of service: 18 days per year
- 5+ years of service: 22 days per year

Leave must be applied for at least 2 weeks in advance for periods
exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
Leave encashment is available upon resignation or retirement.

3. SICK LEAVE
-------------
All employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be used for personal errands or non-medical purposes.

4. CASUAL LEAVE
---------------
Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per year.
Casual leave is for personal emergencies and short-term needs.
Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave.
It should be applied for in advance where possible.

5. MATERNITY LEAVE
------------------
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave
for the first two children. For third child onwards, 12 weeks apply.
Maternity leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.
Extensions may be requested and will be evaluated case by case.

6. PATERNITY LEAVE
------------------
Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave
upon the birth or legal adoption of a child.
Paternity leave must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

7. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
--------------------
In the event of the death of an immediate family member:
- Spouse, child, parent: 5 days paid leave
- Sibling, grandparent, parent-in-law: 3 days paid leave
Additional unpaid leave may be approved by the manager.

8. UNPAID LEAVE
---------------
Employees may apply for unpaid leave for personal reasons.
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be approved by direct managers.
Extended unpaid leave requires HR and department head approval.

9. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
------------------
ACME Technologies observes 12 public holidays per calendar year.
The list of public holidays is published at the start of each year.
If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is a substitute.

10. LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
------------------------------
Apply for leave through the Employee Self Service portal.
Manager approval is required for all leave types.
Emergency leave (same-day) must be communicated by phone or email.
Unapproved absences are treated as Loss of Pay (LOP).

11. LEAVE ABUSE
---------------
Employees found abusing leave policies may face disciplinary action.
Patterns of unplanned leave before or after weekends/holidays will
be reviewed and may require documentation.

For questions, contact HR at hr@acmetech.com or ext. 1200.
